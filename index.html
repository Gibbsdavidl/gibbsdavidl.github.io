About

I'm a computational biologist specializing in AI/ML for digital pathology, spatial transcriptomics, and multi-modal data integration in cancer research.

Currently, I'm developing multi-headed classifiers on the Virchow2 foundation model to simultaneously predict tumor subtypes, stromal architecture, and immune cell populations from histopathology—enabling patient stratification and biomarker discovery for precision medicine.

My work at the Institute for Systems Biology integrates cutting-edge deep learning with traditional bioinformatics to understand tumor-immune interactions across multiple data modalities. I've contributed to major cancer consortiums (NCI CSBC, CRUK Grand Challenges, TCGA PanCancer Atlas) and published almost(!) 40 papers including co-first author on "The Immune Landscape of Cancer" (Immunity, 2019 - 4700+ citations).

I build scientific software, mentor students, and excel at translating complex biological questions into computational solutions through collaboration with pathologists, clinicians, and experimental biologists.

Featured Projects

Pathology Foundation Models for Tumor Microenvironment Analysis

2024-Present

AI/ML | Digital Pathology | Foundation Models

Multi-headed deep learning architecture for simultaneous prediction of cancer subtypes, stromal structures, T cell populations, and macrophage types from H&E whole slide images. Fine-tuning a vision transformer for comprehensive tumor microenvironment characterization.

Technologies: PyTorch, Virchow2, Multi-task learning, Whole slide imaging, Vision Transformers
Impact: Enabling spatial biomarker discovery for patient stratification in immuno-oncology; part of NCI Cancer Systems Biology Consortium

CycleGAN for Histopathology Stain Transformation

2023-2024

Computer Vision | Deep Learning

Custom unpaired image-to-image translation model for IHC-to-H&E conversion, enabling cross-stain analysis. Solved training-inference mismatches in whole slide image processing while preserving nuclear structure across diverse tissue types and staining protocols.

Technologies: PyTorch, CycleGAN, MONAI, Whole slide image processing, Domain adaptation
Impact: Automated stain normalization for large-scale pathology analysis, enabling cross-study comparisons

Spatial Tumor Microenvironment Analysis Pipeline

2022-Present

Spatial Biology | Statistical Methods

Multi-scale statistical framework for quantifying immune cell infiltration patterns using Moran's I spatial autocorrelation with pyramid approaches for different tissue scales. Integrates with WSInfer for cell detection and spatial relationship mapping.

Technologies: Python, scikit-image, Spatial statistics, Digital pathology, Multi-scale analysis
Impact: Revealing tumor-immune spatial architectures predictive of therapy response across cancer types

CRDC Imaging Data Commons
