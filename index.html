01 — Now

Building models for drug discovery at Powerhouse

I'm an Applied AI/ML Scientist at Powerhouse, working on AI/ML for drug design. I bring more than ten years of building models on messy, multi-modal biological data: genomics, single-cell, spatial, and whole-slide pathology.

i.

Target discovery

Mining multi-omics, spatial, and imaging data to nominate and prioritize targets and the biomarkers that go with them, so you know which patients and why.

ii.

Lab-in-the-loop

Active learning that couples models to wet-lab experiments. The model chooses the most informative next batch, the assays come back, and the model gets retrained.

iii.

Methods & model training

New statistical methods plus training and fine-tuning AI models, including foundation models, with the uncertainty estimates and validation that real decisions require.

02 — Background

From the immune landscape to the lab bench

Before Powerhouse, I spent nearly a decade at the Institute for Systems Biology in Seattle. I built pathology foundation-model classifiers, spatial statistics for the tumor microenvironment, and multi-omic integration methods for cancer consortia including NCI CSBC, CRUK Grand Challenge, HTAN, and the TCGA PanCancer Atlas.

I was co-first author on The Immune Landscape of Cancer (Immunity, 2019) and co-PI on the NCI Imaging Data Commons. I like being the person who translates a biological question into a model and then a model into an experiment, working side by side with biologists, chemists, pathologists, and clinicians.

03 — Selected work

Projects

2024 – 2026Foundation models

Multi-headed classifiers on pathology foundation models

Fine-tuned a multi-task head on the Virchow2 vision transformer to simultaneously predict tumor subtype, stromal architecture, T cell, and macrophage populations from H&E whole-slide images, for spatial biomarker discovery in the NCI Cancer Systems Biology Consortium.

PyTorch · Virchow2 · ViTs · multi-task learning · WSI

2025Generative / CV

CycleGAN stain transformation

Unpaired IHC→H&E translation for whole-slide images. We fixed train/inference mismatches while preserving nuclear morphology across tissue types and staining protocols.

PyTorch · CycleGAN · MONAI · domain adaptation

2024 – 2026Spatial statistics

Multi-scale spatial analysis of the tumor microenvironment

Pyramid-based Moran's I and spatial autocorrelation framework that quantifies immune infiltration patterns across tissue scales and links tumor-immune architecture to therapy response.

Python · scikit-image · spatial statistics · WSInfer

2022 – 2026Infrastructure

NCI Imaging Data Commons

Co-PI and technical lead for a 36TB+ cloud-native repository of pathology and radiology data used by cancer researchers worldwide.

GCP · BigQuery · DICOM · OME-TIFF
